## Reading Text

Every weekday morning in the Miller household begins at precisely 6:30 AM when the loud alarm clock goes off. Sarah, an organized high school teacher, believes that maintaining a healthy daily routine is the fundamental secret to a peaceful and productive life. She is a natural early bird who enjoys waking up before sunrise to stretch, open the bedroom window for crisp morning air, and enjoy a quiet cup of hot coffee before the rest of the busy house wakes up.

Her husband, Mark, has a slightly different daily schedule because he works as a freelance graphic designer from his cozy home office. However, being punctual and sharing household responsibilities is very important to both of them. After getting out of bed, they take a warm shower, get dressed quickly, and brush their teeth with mint toothpaste. While Mark prepares a nutritious breakfast of scrambled eggs, whole-wheat toast, and fresh fruit, Sarah helps their two children get dressed and ready for school. Eating a balanced breakfast together at the wooden dining table gives the entire household enough physical and mental energy for their busy day ahead.

Sharing household chores is an essential family rule in their home. No single person is expected to do all the cleaning alone. Sarah washes the breakfast dishes and wipes the kitchen counter, while Mark makes the beds, takes out the trash, and irons clothes for the day. In the afternoon, Mark commutes to the supermarket for weekly grocery shopping. In the evening, the children help sweep and mop the kitchen floor and dust the bookshelves, while Sarah does the laundry.

After school and work, the family gathers for a relaxed dinnertime around 7:00 PM in the dining room. They discuss their weekly goals and mark upcoming school events on their wall calendar. After dinner, they spend their leisure time reading novels, practicing creative hobbies, or going for a gentle evening stroll through the neighborhood park. By 10:00 PM, everyone prepares for bedtime, ensuring they get eight hours of restful sleep without overworking themselves.
