## Reading Text

Our planet Earth is a magnificent sanctuary of natural wonders, shaped by cyclical weather seasons, rich biodiversity, and delicate ecological systems. From the sunny warmth of tropical ocean beaches to the serene silence of snowy pine forests, mountain peaks, and rolling green agricultural valleys, nature provides humanity with life-giving fresh air, clean drinking water, fertile soil, and endless creative inspiration.

The four distinct seasons bring a continuous, dependable rhythm of transformation throughout the year. Spring is a season of rebirth, when delicate green leaves sprout on tall trees and colorful flowers bloom across gardens. Summer brings long sunny days and warm breezes, inviting families and friends to go outdoor swimming, mountain hiking, and weekend camping under the stars. In autumn, the tree foliage transforms into vibrant shades of orange, gold, and red before falling gently to nourish the earth below. Winter blankets the mountains and rooftops with sparkling white snow, reminding us of nature's power, quiet stillness, and cozy hearths.

However, modern industrial activities and urban expansion have placed severe strain on the natural environment. Air and single-use plastic pollution, deforestation, and global climate change threaten fragile wildlife habitats, oceans, and rivers. Rising global temperatures lead to more extreme weather phenomena across continents, including violent thunderstorms with lightning, prolonged agricultural droughts, and dangerous summer heatwaves.

Fortunately, environmental protection begins with conscious daily actions in our own homes and neighborhoods. Every individual can make a tangible difference by adopting simple eco-friendly habits. Reducing single-use plastic, carrying reusable shopping bags and stainless steel water bottles, sorting household trash to recycle glass, metal, and paper, and conserving water at home preserve precious natural resources. Planting trees and flowers in urban streets cleans the atmosphere, produces fresh oxygen, and provides refreshing shade. By respecting nature and embracing sustainable choices, we preserve our shared Earth for future generations.
