### Day 01: Personal Identity, Lifestyle & Habits
1. **Q001 (Warm-up)**: How would you describe your typical morning routine, and how does it set the tone for the rest of your day?
2. **Q002 (Warm-up)**: What is one daily habit or ritual that you consider indispensable to your peace of mind?
3. **Q003 (Warm-up)**: Are you naturally an early bird or a night owl, and have you ever attempted to alter this tendency?
4. **Q004 (Warm-up)**: When you have an unexpected free afternoon, what activities do you instinctively gravitate toward?
5. **Q005 (Warm-up)**: How has your definition of a "successful day" evolved over the past five years?
6. **Q006 (In-Depth)**: To what extent do your daily habits align with the long-term aspirations you hold for your personal and professional life?
7. **Q007 (In-Depth)**: In what ways does your physical living space reflect your personality, mental state, and aesthetic sensibilities?
8. **Q008 (In-Depth)**: When facing burnout or cognitive fatigue, what specific restorative practices do you employ to regain equilibrium?
9. **Q009 (In-Depth)**: How do you maintain boundaries between your professional obligations and personal leisure in an increasingly connected world?
10. **Q010 (In-Depth)**: Which trait or habit have you inherited from your family or cultural upbringing that you cherish or actively try to modify?
11. **Q011 (Debate/Abstract)**: Some philosophers argue that we are merely the cumulative total of our automated habits. Do you agree or disagree? Why?
12. **Q012 (Debate/Abstract)**: How does the modern obsession with hyper-productivity and "hustle culture" undermine genuine personal fulfillment?
13. **Q013 (Debate/Abstract)**: Is personal identity predominantly shaped by external societal conditioning or intrinsic biological and psychological factors?
14. **Q014 (Debate/Abstract)**: In the digital era, do social media personas allow people to express their authentic identity or foster artificial facades?
15. **Q015 (Debate/Abstract)**: If you could instantaneously eliminate one pervasive habit from human society, which would it be, and what societal transformation would follow?

### Day 02: Travel, Cross-Cultural Exploration & Tourism
16. **Q016 (Warm-up)**: What was your most memorable vacation or journey, and what made it stand out in your memory?
17. **Q017 (Warm-up)**: When traveling, do you prefer a meticulously organized itinerary or impromptu, spontaneous wandering?
18. **Q018 (Warm-up)**: What is the most exotic or unfamiliar dish you have ever sampled while abroad?
19. **Q019 (Warm-up)**: What essential item—aside from your passport and phone—can you never travel without?
20. **Q020 (Warm-up)**: Would you rather explore a bustling metropolitan hub or retreat to a secluded natural sanctuary?
21. **Q021 (In-Depth)**: Can you recount an instance where you experienced profound culture shock or a humorous cross-cultural misunderstanding?
22. **Q022 (In-Depth)**: What are the psychological advantages and challenges of undertaking solo travel in an unfamiliar foreign environment?
23. **Q023 (In-Depth)**: How does immersing yourself in a culture drastically different from your own challenge your ingrained assumptions?
24. **Q024 (In-Depth)**: In what ways has budget travel (such as hostels, backpacking, and ridesharing) democratized global exploration?
25. **Q025 (In-Depth)**: If you were appointed as the tourism ambassador for your home country, how would you promote its hidden treasures to international travelers?
26. **Q026 (Debate/Abstract)**: How can popular destinations strike an ethical balance between reaping economic benefits from tourism and preventing severe cultural erosion?
27. **Q027 (Debate/Abstract)**: Does modern hyper-connectivity and GPS technology strip travel of its authentic serendipity and adventurous spirit?
28. **Q028 (Debate/Abstract)**: Should affluent nations restrict the influx of tourists visiting fragile ecosystems, or is global exploration a universal right?
29. **Q029 (Debate/Abstract)**: What is the distinction between being an ethical, conscientious "traveler" versus a superficial, extractive "tourist"?
30. **Q030 (Debate/Abstract)**: Do you believe that widespread global tourism actively dismantles geopolitical prejudices and fosters world peace?

### Day 03: Gastronomy, Dining & Culinary Traditions

32. **Q032 (Warm-up)**: Do you consider cooking to be a relaxing, creative art form or an exhausting, repetitive chore?

39. **Q039 (In-Depth)**: How has the ubiquity of food delivery apps altered eating habits, culinary appreciation, and cooking skills?

41. **Q041 (Debate/Abstract)**: Does the global proliferation of fast-food chains threaten to homogenize and obliterate authentic indigenous food cultures?
