### 🎯 Pedagogical Design of Each Daily Module
Every day is structured into 6 sequential, communicative sections:
1. **Module Overview & Learning Objectives**: Clear communicative goals (Can-Do statements).
2. **Warm-Up & Icebreaker Questions (5 Questions)**: Low-friction questions to build confidence and fluency.
3. **In-Depth Situational & Analytical Questions (5 Questions)**: Moderate-complexity inquiries requiring justification, examples, and storytelling.
4. **Abstract Debate & Critical Thinking Questions (5 Questions)**: High-level questions testing nuance, conditional phrasing, and complex opinions.
5. **Target Vocabulary Lexicon (50 Words per Day)**:
   - Part of Speech (noun, verb, adj, adv)
   - IPA Phonetic Transcription
   - Clear CEFR B2-C2 definitions
   - Natural, high-utility example sentences
   - Essential Collocations & Prepositional pairings
6. **Idiomatic Expressions & Conversational Formulas**: Common idioms and conversational linking phrases.
7. **Real-World Speaking Activity / Roleplay Scenario**: Guided interactive practice for pair or group work.
